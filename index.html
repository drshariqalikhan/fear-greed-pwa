Breakout-Breakdown Drivers

Underlying inputs dictating the breakout/breakdown probability.

1. Market Maker Positioning (Inverse Put/Call) Analyzing...

MMs profit by taking opposite side of retail calls/puts. High call demand $\implies$ MMs favor breakdown.

2. Price Action Float Phase (Rolling 30-Day SPY) Analyzing...

Rolling 30-day float up consolidation favors break out; float down consolidation favors breakdown.

3. Safe Haven Demand (Flight to Treasuries) Analyzing...

Rising flight to Treasuries signals breakdown; risk-on rotation into stocks signals breakout.
